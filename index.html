Data Centre Power & Sales Monitoring

Two interactive Plotly treemaps for monitoring power capacity utilisation and colocation capacity sales. The dashboards show where actual load differs from available capacity and where sales commitments may require commercial or operational review.

27 data centres
113 server halls
130 MW projected capacity
60% average utilisation

Interactive dashboards

Open either dashboard to explore the original treemap, drill into locations and inspect individual server halls. The charts and their underlying structure have been retained.

Business context & findings

Operational visibility

Treemap hierarchy allows capacity monitoring from the data centre level down to individual server halls.

Capacity pressure

Some halls show consumption or sold commitments above nominal capacity, prompting targeted operational review.

Commercial opportunities

Low-utilisation facilities stand out for sales pipeline analysis and capacity allocation decisions.

Methods and tools: Python · Pandas · SQLite · Plotly · Hierarchical treemap visualisation · GitHub Pages. This public project uses demonstration data and is independent of confidential employer reporting.
